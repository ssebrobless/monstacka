 MonStacka Block Ability Reference
Draft for player-facing rewrite and voice/script review

Purpose:
This document includes both:
1. The short text currently shown in the home menu block previewer.
2. The real gameplay details behind each friendly and enemy ability.

Friendly abilities are used by the player.
Enemy abilities are Story Mode mission modifiers.

============================================================
SHARED FRIENDLY ABILITY RULES
============================================================

Where friendly abilities are enabled:
- Story Mode: friendly abilities are always enabled.
- O.G.B.M.: Zany enables friendly abilities. Classic disables them.
- X(4)-LINES: Zany enables friendly abilities. Classic disables them.
- Training: friendly abilities can be toggled on/off in the match screen. Toggling resets the board.

Friendly ability trigger:
- Put a block in Hold.
- Bring that held block back out later.
- Place that block.
- Do that 3 times total.
- When the Hold box glows, the next block placed from Hold fires its ability.

Combo rule:
- A new trigger within 6 total placements of the previous trigger keeps the combo chain.
- Combo bonus is +100 points per combo step after the first trigger.

Shared friendly point values:
- +150 base points when an assist triggers.
- +100 for each combo step.
- +300 danger-save bonus when applicable.
- +200 per line cleared during an active 8-second timed assist window.

Danger rule:
- A stack is considered dangerous when blocks reach the upper danger zone, currently about 14 visible rows high.
- Some abilities award an extra danger-save bonus if they help while the stack is dangerous.

============================================================
AGGRASO / Z
============================================================

FRIENDLY ABILITY: GUARD BREAK

Home menu preview text:
When this triggers, the game removes up to 6 enemy junk cells from your stack. It checks low rows first, then the left and right edges.

Home menu trigger text:
Trigger: put a block in Hold, bring it back out later, then place it. Do that 3 times. When the Hold box glows, the next block placed from Hold fires this ability.

Home menu points text:
+150 when it triggers. +20 for each junk cell removed. +300 extra if it saves you while the stack is dangerous. +100 for each combo step.

Actual friendly mechanics:
- Triggered by placing the 3rd held-deployment block.
- Removes up to 6 enemy junk/garbage cells.
- Board cleanup prioritizes garbage cells lower in the stack and near the left/right edges.
- Awards +20 per garbage cell removed.
- Awards +300 danger-save bonus if the stack was dangerous and at least one garbage cell was removed.

ENEMY ABILITY: GUARD PRESSURE

Home menu preview text:
Story effect: Guard Pressure raises the stack with timed bottom rows.

Actual enemy modifier:
- GuardPressure

Enemy trigger details:
- GuardPressure is active for the whole mission when the chapter includes that modifier.
- It runs on a timer that gets shorter as difficulty tier rises.
- When the timer fills, it adds a full pressure row at the bottom of the stack.
- Each pressure row lasts 6 seconds, or can be removed early by clearing a row.
- Clearing a row removes only the oldest active Guard Pressure row.
- Signal Relay can temporarily reactivate GuardPressure in later story missions.

MUWERDE / O
============================================================

FRIENDLY ABILITY: CALCULATION

Home menu preview text:
When this triggers, the Next list shows 2 extra upcoming blocks for 8 seconds. Use it to plan farther ahead.

Home menu trigger text:
Trigger: put a block in Hold, bring it back out later, then place it. Do that 3 times. When the Hold box glows, the next block placed from Hold fires this ability.

Home menu points text:
+150 when it triggers. +200 for each line cleared before the 8-second window ends. +300 extra for a danger save when applicable. +100 for each combo step.

Actual friendly mechanics:
- Triggered by placing the 3rd held-deployment block.
- Starts an 8-second CALCULATION window.
- During the window, the Next queue shows 2 extra upcoming blocks.
- Awards +200 per line cleared while the window is active.
- CALCULATION rewards planning and line clears during its window. It does not give an instant danger-save payout by itself.

ENEMY ABILITY: CALCULATED

Home menu preview text:
Story effect: Muwerde missions focus on planning. Some give a longer Next list, then punish messy placement patterns like overhang-heavy stacks.

Actual enemy modifiers:
- CalculatedPlanning
- PrecisionPressure

Enemy trigger details:
- CalculatedPlanning is active for the whole mission when a Muwerde chapter includes it.
- It can increase the Next preview count through the chapter spec so the player can plan farther ahead.
- It tracks successful rotations on every piece until a score debuff is already queued.
- Rotating more than 3 times queues a score debuff for the next block the player locks.
- The over-rotated block is not debuffed; the next placed block is.
- If a debuff is queued, extra rotations do not queue another debuff until that next block locks.
- Rows cleared with any cell from the debuffed block award reduced points, which also reduces mission HP damage.
- The reduction is 30% plus 5% per difficulty tier, capped at 75%.
- PrecisionPressure is active for the whole mission when a Muwerde chapter includes it.
- When a piece locks, the game checks the cells in that piece.
- Any locked cell that hangs over empty space, without another cell in that same piece below it, counts as an unsupported overhang.
- Unsupported overhangs seed enemy territory cells, up to 3 penalty cells per locked piece.
- The right-side story HUD reports current rotation pressure and the most recent Muerde penalty status during missions.

Home menu trigger text:

Actual friendly mechanics:

- Starts an 8-second ECHO GUIDE window.

- Awards +200 per line cleared while the window is active.

ENEMY ABILITY: BLINDED

Home menu preview text:
Story effect: placed blocks flicker in and out on a timer, making it harder to read the stack while you keep playing.

Actual enemy modifiers:
- Blinded

Enemy trigger details:
- Blinded runs on a 12-second cooldown.
- When the cooldown fills, Blinded activates for 4 to 7 seconds depending on story difficulty.
- While active, every placed block on the board flickers between visible and invisible every 0.5 seconds.
- The falling active piece stays visible.
- Blinded cannot be canceled early.
- When Blinded ends, all placed blocks are forced visible again even if the last flicker state was invisible.
- Signal Relay can temporarily reactivate Blinded in later story missions.

DOUSEMA / J
============================================================

FRIENDLY ABILITY: SCRAPS

Home menu preview text:
When this triggers, the game searches for the deepest empty cell with blocks above it and fills that hole with a repair cell.

Home menu trigger text:
Trigger: put a block in Hold, bring it back out later, then place it. Do that 3 times. When the Hold box glows, the next block placed from Hold fires this ability.

Home menu points text:
+150 when it triggers. +120 if it successfully fills a hole. +100 for each combo step.

Actual friendly mechanics:
- Triggered by placing the 3rd held-deployment block.
- Searches the board for the deepest covered hole.
- A covered hole is an empty cell with blocks above it.
- Fills that hole with a repair cell.
- Awards +120 if a hole was successfully repaired.

ENEMY ABILITY: RESILIENT CELLS

Home menu preview text:
Story effect: one permanent claimed source spreads to adjacent locked blocks. Claimed blocks cannot help complete row clears until you clear other rows to remove the claims.

Actual enemy modifiers:
- ResilientCells
- MutedHints
- NoHold in some later story chapters

Enemy trigger details:
- ResilientCells triggers at match start when the chapter includes that modifier.
- It creates one permanent claimed source that stays for the whole match.
- Its timer gets shorter as difficulty tier rises.
- When the timer fills, it claims one random locked block touching the permanent source or another claimed block.
- Claimed blocks do not count toward row completion.
- Clearing a row removes one temporary Resilient Cells claim, oldest first. The permanent source remains.
- MutedHints is active for the whole mission when the chapter includes it.
- It hides assist/status hints.
- NoHold is applied by chapter rules when included, disabling Hold for that mission.

Home menu trigger text:

ENEMY ABILITY: INSATIABLE HUNGER

Home menu preview text:
Counts player-cleared lines. When the requirement is met, it eats one whole block from the highest occupied layer of the board.

Actual enemy modifier:
- HungerMeter (legacy internal modifier id)

Enemy trigger details:
- Insatiable Hunger is active for the whole mission when the chapter includes HungerMeter.
- Early missions trigger after 3 player-cleared lines.
- Difficulty scaling reduces that requirement: higher tiers can trigger after 2 cleared lines, then every cleared line.
- The line counter carries leftover progress. Example: if the requirement is 2 and you clear 3 lines, one trigger fires and 1 line of progress remains.
- The effect resolves after the player's row clear finishes and the stack collapses.
- It targets the highest occupied player block layer on the board.
- It chooses one block from that top layer and removes every remaining cell that belongs to that same block.
- If the target block had already been broken by a previous row clear, every surviving cell from that block is still removed.

LYSERGICADA / T
============================================================

FRIENDLY ABILITY: SEDATION

Home menu preview text:
When this triggers, falling speed slows for 8 seconds and lock delay gets longer, giving you more time before a piece sticks.

Home menu trigger text:
Trigger: put a block in Hold, bring it back out later, then place it. Do that 3 times. When the Hold box glows, the next block placed from Hold fires this ability.

Home menu points text:
+150 when it triggers. +200 for each line cleared before the 8-second window ends. +300 extra for a danger save when applicable. +100 for each combo step.

Actual friendly mechanics:
- Triggered by placing the 3rd held-deployment block.
- Starts an 8-second SEDATE window.
- Gravity multiplier becomes 1.6, which means slower falling.
- Lock delay gets +0.15 seconds.
- Awards +200 per line cleared while the window is active.
- SEDATE rewards line clears during its slowdown window. It does not give an instant danger-save payout by itself.

ENEMY ABILITY: SEDATING SPIT

Home menu preview text:
Story effect: a timed spit attack wipes friendly assist charge and blocks new assist progress until it ends.

Actual enemy modifier:
- SedationWindows

Enemy trigger details:
- SedationWindows is active for the whole mission when the chapter includes it.
- Sedating Spit runs on a 15-second cooldown.
- When it activates, it lasts 4 to 8 seconds depending on story difficulty.
- On activation, any active friendly assist window is cleared.
- On activation, any held-piece assist progress or ready held-assist charge is reset to 0/3.
- While active, friendly assists cannot trigger.
- While active, held-piece placements do not gain progress toward the next friendly assist.
- Clearing a row ends Sedating Spit early.
- If no row is cleared, Sedating Spit ends when its active duration runs out and then starts its cooldown again.

BLYNDOOLIE / I
============================================================

FRIENDLY ABILITY: ALERT

Home menu preview text:
When this triggers, an 8-second danger window starts. If your stack is high during that window, the ability can award a danger-save bonus.

Home menu trigger text:
Trigger: put a block in Hold, bring it back out later, then place it. Do that 3 times. When the Hold box glows, the next block placed from Hold fires this ability.

Home menu points text:
+150 when it triggers. +200 for each line cleared before the 8-second window ends. +300 extra for a danger save when applicable. +100 for each combo step.

Actual friendly mechanics:
- Triggered by placing the 3rd held-deployment block.
- Starts an 8-second ALERT window.
- If the stack was already dangerous when ALERT triggered, it awards +300 danger-save bonus.
- During the window, clears made while the stack is dangerous can receive an Alert score multiplier of 1.5.
- Awards +200 per line cleared while the window is active.

ENEMY ABILITY: ADRENALINE RUSH

Home menu preview text:
Adrenaline Rush periodically boosts enemy abilities. Effects that start during Rush keep their boosted version until they finish.

Actual enemy modifiers:
- AdrenalineMonitor (legacy internal modifier id)
- SignalRelay

Enemy trigger details:
- Adrenaline Rush is active for the whole mission when the chapter includes AdrenalineMonitor.
- Rush waits 20 seconds, activates for 11 seconds, then repeats.
- Rush cannot be ended early by line clears or player actions.
- While Rush is active, enemy abilities that trigger use boosted difficulty scaling.
- Timed effects that start during Rush keep their boosted duration until they finish, even if Rush ends first.
- Once Rush ends, future enemy ability cycles return to their normal unboosted scaling until the next Rush window.
- SignalRelay is active in later chapters when declared.
- Signal Relay waits 25 seconds, then activates for 6 seconds.
- When it activates, it randomly chooses one earlier modifier:
  GuardPressure, ResilientCells, Blinded, or Adrenaline Rush.
- If it chooses ResilientCells, it seeds a permanent source if none exists, or claims one touching block if a source already exists.
- After 6 seconds, the relay turns off and the 25-second timer starts again.

============================================================
STORY BOSS MISSION MODEL
============================================================

Story missions are treated as boss fights.
- Score gained during the match damages the mission health bar.
- The mission clears when boss HP reaches 0.
- The mission fails if the player tops out.
- The mission also fails if the mission timer reaches 0 on timed boss missions.

Difficulty can scale through:
- larger boss HP values
- tighter mission timers
- faster gravity
- shorter lock delay
- reduced Next preview count
- Hold disabled
- monster-specific hazards
